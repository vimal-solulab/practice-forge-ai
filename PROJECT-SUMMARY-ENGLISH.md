# PracticeOS AI — Project Summary

## What is it?
PracticeOS AI is an **AI Operating System** built for Accounting, Tax and Advisory firms. It runs the entire firm from one platform — from client onboarding all the way to advisory — with AI working across every step.

## Who is it for (Use Case)
- Accounting, tax and advisory firms managing many clients
- Firms still running the business through inboxes, spreadsheets and disconnected software
- Partners/Managers who need one place to see the whole firm's status

## What it's used for (Core Features)
1. **Client 360** — full profile of every client (individuals, companies, trusts, SMSFs, etc.) in one place
2. **Job & Workflow** — track every job (BAS, tax returns, etc.), deadlines and staff allocation
3. **Documents & Portal** — request, upload and approve client documents in one place
4. **Workpapers & Reconciliation** — prepare bank reconciliations, fixed assets, GST workpapers
5. **Tax & Compliance** — manage BAS, GST, income tax and lodgement deadlines
6. **Billing & WIP** — track time, billing, staff utilisation and client profitability

## What the AI does
- **AI Workpaper Agent** — turns a folder of raw documents into a review-ready workpaper file automatically (e.g. 91% job completion in the example)
- **AI Client Chaser** — automatically follows up with clients for missing documents instead of the accountant chasing manually
- **AI Review & Advisory Copilot** — spots unusual patterns in client financials (e.g. shrinking margins) and surfaces advisory opportunities
- **AI Practice Command Centre** — partners can ask things like "which jobs will miss deadline?" and get instant answers plus suggested actions

## The benefit (in one line)
Accountants focus on judgement and advisory work — AI handles the repetitive work (chasing, data entry, reconciliation) around every engagement. This lets a firm grow its client base without growing headcount at the same rate.
